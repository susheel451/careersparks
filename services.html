What we do

Our Services

Job consultancy, career guidance, teaching support and community programmes.

Service 01

Job Consultancy

Job consultancy plays a key role in searching for talent from a large applicant pool that accurately matches a client's job description and requirements. In doing so, the consultants also prove beneficial to job seekers.

While exploring the best match for their clients' needs, job consultancy helps job seekers discover more suitable employment opportunities, enhance their CV, improve their skill sets through professional training, and gain many more advantages during their journey with the placement consultancy at Career Spark in Mysore.

Enquire about job consultancy
Service 02

Career-Path Guidance for Job Seekers

The most significant benefit of a job consultancy to a job seeker is the exceptional advice consultants can provide on career opportunities. Our professional consultants analyse the qualifications, experience, skills and requirements of candidates, and advise and guide them on the career opportunities that existing clients are offering.

Career Spark guides job seekers in finding alternative career paths that could be more beneficial for them in the future. We consider the interests and preferences of candidates to identify more favourable opportunities in their desired industry.

Community

Vanamala Foundation & Educational Trust

Vanamala Foundation is a registered charity. Prasad S. M. is the president of the foundation.

The charitable trust conducts many awareness programmes for children, women and farmers, and helps people suffering from poverty. It conducts free consulting classes for children, women, families, and school and college students, and works towards establishing consulting and educational centres for poor people all over India.

The trust helps people affected by disasters such as floods, earthquakes and COVID-19, and donates basic necessities like food and clothes to those in need.

  • Awareness programmes such as quiz programmes
  • Communication English training for students
  • Free books for students
  • Job training for poor students

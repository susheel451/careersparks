About Career Spark

Experiential career mentoring and guidance for school and college students.

Who we are

A holistic and unbiased career ecosystem

Career Spark is an experiential career mentoring and guidance venture that builds a holistic and unbiased career ecosystem.

We work towards helping school and college students bring clarity to their career choices, and assist them to develop the relevant skills and mindset required to excel in their chosen field.

  • Career clarity for school and college students
  • Skill and mindset development
  • Main branch in Mysore, Vijayanagara 1st Stage; branches in Bengaluru, Tumkur and Chikmagalur

Managing Director

Dr. Prasad S. M.

Managing Director

Dr. Prasad S. M. is the Managing Director of Career Spark. He has been associated with the company since its inception as promoter.

Roles

  • Managing Director, Career Spark Edu Solutions & Technologies (R)
  • Founder & President, Vanamala Foundation & Educational & Charitable Trust (R)
  • Managing Director, LPS Ventures

Qualifications

  • M.A. in English & Economics
  • Counsellor in Psychology
  • Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)
  • Post Graduate Diploma in English (P.G.D.E.)
  • Diploma in Computer Application (D.C.A.)
  • Post Graduate Diploma in Journalism (P.G.D.J)

Dr. Prasad has been honoured in various programmes for his support of the Kannada and English languages. He also has his own film production house.

He is the Founder and Chairperson for the overall working of Career Spark and Royal Dreamz, and is instrumental in making strategic decisions for the companies.

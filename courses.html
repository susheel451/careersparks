Associated academy

👗 Royal Dreamz Fashion Modelling and Acting Academy

Fashion modelling, events and acting school in Mysore with diploma certification.

Fashion Modelling

Duration: 3 months

Acting

Duration: 6 months

Fashion Events Course & Programmes

Training on commercials, ramp-walk modelling, catalogue modelling and voice overs.

Eligibility: 8th, 9th, 10th (pass or fail), PUC, Degree. For more information, contact Career Spark.

Royal Dreamz offers courses in fashion modelling, events and acting in Mysore with diploma certification and job opportunities, including job placements at various companies.

Modelling courses lead candidates to a glamorous career dominated by physical attributes and personal qualities, and are also considered an initial step towards acting in film and television. They usually teach candidates about the modelling industry, dos and don'ts as a model, how to prepare portfolios, runway and fashion parade training, posing techniques, television modelling, photographic workshops, model make-up, and backstage standards and ethics.

Fashion modelling uses people to show off a particular type of fashion, helping the public see how it looks and creating interest. Items that can be modelled include garments, accessories, jewellery and cosmetics. In the fashion industry, modelling is often used as a type of advertising, with models featured in magazines and catalogues, on television and on the Internet.
